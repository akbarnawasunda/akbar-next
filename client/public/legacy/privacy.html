Privacy Policy

THE SHORT VERSION

This site is built to be as light on your data as possible. No advertising, no tracking cookies, no selling data — ever. Everything below is the complete, honest list of what happens when you use this site.

WHAT WE COLLECT

  • Fan Signal. If you subscribe, your email address is stored in the site's subscriber database and used only for official release, visual, and live updates. You may request removal anytime by emailing us.
  • Contact / collab form. Your name, email, project context, and message are stored in the site's inquiry database so the artist can review and respond to the request.
  • Analytics. A privacy-friendly analytics script may be loaded when the site owner has configured it. It is intended to count visits in aggregate and does not use advertising cookies.
  • Browser storage. The site may store limited interface preferences or authentication-support data in your browser's localStorage. This information is not used for advertising.

COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. Limited localStorage may be used for interface preferences and authentication-support data.

Music players (Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud) are click-to-load: nothing is loaded from those platforms until you explicitly tap play. After you click, their own privacy policies apply.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

  • Vercel — hosting & delivery
  • Google Fonts — typefaces
  • Umami — cookieless analytics
  • Site backend and database — subscriber and inquiry storage used to operate the official site
  • Spotify / YouTube / SoundCloud — embedded players (only after your click)
  • Apple iTunes Search — cover art & 30s previews

Each service has its own privacy policy.

YOUR RIGHTS

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold (e.g. your Fan Signal email or inquiry) at any time by contacting akbarnawasunda@gmail.com. This applies including under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27/2022) and, for visitors in the EU/EEA, the GDPR.
